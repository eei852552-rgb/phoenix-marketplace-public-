# Privacy Notice — Phoenix Marketplace Club

**Status:** Demo notice, not a final legal document. It must be reviewed and updated before collecting personal information or launching marketplace accounts.

## Current demo
This public website is a static front-end demonstration. It does not currently provide account registration, seller submissions, checkout, messaging or customer support forms. Do not enter passwords, payment details, identity documents or other private information into this demo.

The sample product listings are illustrative and are not real offers for sale.

## Hosting and technical information
The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as request logs, IP addresses, browser information and security events as part of operating and protecting the service. Consult the hosting provider's current privacy documentation for details about its processing and retention.

## Future marketplace
Before any account or transaction feature is enabled, this notice must identify:
- The legal entity/operator and a working privacy contact
- What personal information is collected and why
- The lawful basis/consent mechanism where required
- Backend, hosting, messaging and payment service providers
- Retention periods, deletion and account-closure process
- Security safeguards and incident notification process
- User rights and how to exercise them
- Any cross-border processing and applicable safeguards

## Children and sensitive information
The demo is not designed to collect information from children. Do not submit sensitive personal information. Any future age requirements and safeguards must be reviewed for applicable law before launch.

## Changes and contact
This notice must be updated before new data collection begins. A real privacy contact must be published before the marketplace accepts user accounts.
