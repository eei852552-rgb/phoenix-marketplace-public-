# Terms of Use — Phoenix Marketplace Club

**Status:** Demo-stage draft only. These terms require review by a qualified local professional before a real marketplace launch.

## 1. Demo status
This website is a demonstration interface. Product names, descriptions, seller names and prices are sample content only. They are not genuine listings, offers, stock information or invitations to purchase.

## 2. No transactions
Accounts, seller registration, product submissions, orders, checkout, payments, delivery tracking and messaging are not active. Do not send money or share private information based on this demo.

## 3. Future marketplace rules
Before launch, the operator must publish clear rules covering:
- Accurate listings, ownership and permission to sell
- Prohibited, illegal, counterfeit, unsafe or restricted goods and services
- Transparent prices, fees, commissions, delivery terms and advertising labels
- Seller verification and buyer responsibilities
- Order acceptance, cancellation, returns, refunds and disputes
- Reviews, moderation decisions, appeals and account suspension
- Fraud, harassment, spam and misuse reporting

## 4. Safety
Users should independently verify sellers and product claims before any future transaction. The operator must provide a functioning report and support process before enabling user-generated listings or messaging.

## 5. Availability and changes
The demo may change or become unavailable. No service-level commitment is made for this prototype.

## 6. Contact and governing law
A real operator identity, support contact, governing law and dispute process must be inserted and reviewed before launch. These details are intentionally not invented in this draft.
